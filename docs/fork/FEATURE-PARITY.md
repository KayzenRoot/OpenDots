implementation** | **CopilotKit dependency** | **Replacement
implementation** | **Tests/verification** | **Known limitations**.

Status is embedded in each cell as needed. "Replacement implementation" names
real files where they exist, and marks PLANNED items with their work order.

  the parity regression net.
- **New tests** are defined in [TEST-PLAN.md](./TEST-PLAN.md) §5 and are PLANNED
  under WORK-009. A feature row is not "verified" until its new test exists and
  passes.
